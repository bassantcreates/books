Quick answers

Before you buy

I haven't done a puzzle since school. Will it be too hard?

Not at all. Start with a word search and move to Sudoku or cryptograms when you feel like it. Answers are included if you get stuck.

Is the print easy to read?

Yes. The Winter and Christmas puzzle books have large-print grids, so you can settle in without squinting.

Do I need an app, a charger or Wi-Fi?

Nope. They're paperbacks. All you need is a pencil (or colored pencils for the coloring books).

Which book is right for my kids?

The Christmas Activity Book is made for ages 7–10. The coloring books suit teens and adults (ages 12+), and the puzzle books are for adults and teens.

Are they good gifts?

Yes. They're easy to wrap, and they get used. The Activity Book makes a great stocking stuffer.
